Micro wedding decor, answered

How many guests is a micro wedding?

Most micro weddings run under 50 guests, and many are 10–25. Our styling scales to fit an intimate ceremony without losing the impact of a larger celebration.

Is decor for a smaller wedding less of an investment?

Yes — our Micro Wedding Styling Package starts at $1,999 for guest counts around 20, with a Full Bloom option at $3,500 for a more complete reception build-out. Both are designed specifically for this scale, not scaled down from a larger package.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A signed agreement and a non-refundable retainer are required to reserve your event date.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 2–6 months out for a micro wedding, though we can often accommodate shorter timelines depending on the season.

Do you travel to backyards or private homes?

Yes — micro weddings often happen outside traditional venues, and we regularly set up in backyards, private estates, and small event spaces throughout Charlotte.
