Baby shower styling, answered

Do you style at-home showers or only venues?

Both. We regularly style living rooms, backyards, and small venues, as well as full event spaces.

How far in advance should I book?

4 to 8 weeks is comfortable for most showers, though we can often accommodate shorter timelines.

Do you deliver and pick up the decor?

Yes, delivery, setup, and breakdown are included in our shower packages.
